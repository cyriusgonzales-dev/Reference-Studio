One search. Four ways to show it.

Type once and every tab searches the same words. Switch tabs to pick what goes on screen.

Images

Click any Google Images result and it's on screen. The full-size picture replaces the thumbnail automatically.

YouTube

Click a video to play it with YouTube's controls. The output shows only the picture, in sync, with the sound.

Lyrics

Highlight the lyrics on the page and they become clean slides. Step through them with the arrow keys. Song credits are shown when the page names them.

Research

Highlight text on a trusted page and it's shown with its source: site, title and date, read from the page itself and never guessed.

Built for live production.

Made for operators who can't afford surprises in the middle of a show.

  • Full HD NDI output with audio. 1920×1080 at up to 30 fps, with the video's sound on the same input.
  • Keeps running in the background. The output never freezes when the window is covered or minimised.
  • Slides that match your show. Choose the font, colours, position and lines per slide, or one-click looks like lower third and green screen for chroma key.
  • One-key black. Clear the output instantly from anywhere in the app.
  • Many computers, one network. Give each computer its own NDI name so vMix tells them apart.
  • Fewer distractions. Built-in ad and tracker blocking, with one click to turn it off.
  • Updates that wait for you. New versions download quietly and install only when you restart, never during a show.
  • Honest sources. Research text always carries where it came from. Text with no single source, such as AI summaries, is refused.

Questions, answered.

What do I need to use it?

vMix on the production computer, and NDI on the computer running this app. vMix installs NDI for you, so on a vMix computer there's nothing extra to install. On another computer, install the free NDI Tools from ndi.video.

vMix is Windows-only. Why is there a Mac version?

NDI works over your local network. Run the app on a Mac, and the vMix computer on the same network will see it as an NDI source under Add Input → NDI.

How do I connect it to vMix?

Start the app, then in vMix choose Add Input → NDI / Desktop Capture and pick YOUR-PC (Vmix Reference Studio). Turn on that input's audio in the vMix mixer to hear videos.

How do updates work?

The app checks for new versions in the background and downloads them quietly. It never restarts by itself: the update installs when you click Restart and install in Settings → Updates, or the next time you quit. You can turn automatic checks and downloads off in Settings.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

Windows shows this for new apps that don't have a paid code-signing certificate yet. Click More info → Run anyway. You only need to do it once.

macOS says the app "can't be opened". What do I do?

Open the Applications folder, right-click (or Control-click) Vmix Reference Studio and choose Open, then Open again. If macOS still refuses, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

Can I show song lyrics on a public broadcast?

Lyrics belong to their publishers. Showing them publicly may require a licence (for example CCLI for churches). Getting that licence is the operator's responsibility.
